How it works

Focus for a while, then breathe

The pomodoro technique splits your work into short stretches. Pomoluna keeps the time, so you only think about what you're doing. The first time, a short tour shows you around.

A pomodoro

25 minutes of focus and 5 of rest. After every four focuses, a long break. You choose the times.

The task

Before you start, tap the + to pick a category and write what you'll do.

Your day, painted

Each focus adds to your daily goal and fills your sky a little more. At a glance you see where your time went.

Focus mode

Tap the sky twice and only the number stays. Nothing to distract you.

Make it yours

Your pace, your things

Your times

Focuses of up to two hours and breaks of up to half an hour, and after how many focuses the long break comes.

Your categories

Each with its own name and color, so you see your day painted by what you did.

An alarm you'll hear

Pick its sound, how loud it gets, how long it rings and how strong it buzzes. It starts soft and rises, so you hear it across the room.

On your home screen

The widget shows the time left over your sky. Pause or skip without opening the app, in the size you like.

Your history, with you

Save a copy of your history to a file and bring it back later, for example on a new phone.

Always in sight

The countdown stays with you in the notification, even with the app closed.

Themes

Pick your sky

Skies that move and fill up with your focuses, or quiet plain colors, light or dark. Change it whenever you like.

Skies · they move and grow with your focuses

Plain colors · quiet, light or dark

Your goals

A little every day

Set a daily goal that fits you. The streak shows how many days in a row you met it, and a joker covers a hard day.

And if you like it, there's more

Every week of your streak earns you a prize drawn in your sky, and a big one each month. You can collect them in every sky, or ignore them: the app works the same.
