How it works

Focus for a while, then breathe

The pomodoro technique splits your work into short stretches. Pomoluna keeps the time, so you only think about what you're doing.

A pomodoro

25 minutes of focus and 5 of rest. After every four focuses, a long break. You choose the times.

The task

Before you start, tap the + to pick a category and write what you'll do.

Your day, painted

Each focus adds to your daily goal and fills your sky a little more. At a glance you see where your time went.

Focus mode

Tap the sky twice and only the number stays. Nothing to distract you.

Your goals

A little every day

Set a daily goal that fits you. The streak shows how many days in a row you met it, and a joker covers a hard day.

And if you like it, there's more

Each week of streak wins you a prize drawn in your sky, and a big one each month. You can collect them in every sky, or ignore them: the app works the same.
