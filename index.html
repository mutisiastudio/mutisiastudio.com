How it works

Focus for a while, then breathe

The pomodoro technique splits your work into short stretches. Pomoluna keeps the time, so you only think about what you're doing.

A pomodoro

25 minutes of focus and 5 of rest. After every four focuses, a long break. You choose the times.

Your sky fills up

Each focus today fills your sky a little more. You see how far you've come without looking at numbers.

The task

Before you start, tap the + to pick a category and write what you'll do.

Focus mode

Tap the sky twice and only the number stays. Nothing to distract you.
